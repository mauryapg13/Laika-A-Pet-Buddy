# Laika: 90-second pitch

**0–45 s: live demo** (app open on the phone; the LEGO hub and camera in view)

> This is Pablo's wall. He pulls the strap. Six good tugs, and Laika drops one treat. Pulling harder never earns more.
> The camera saw him. Claude looked at the frames: "tugging, not eating."
> Now watch the diary. Claude is writing it live, in Pablo's voice, only from what really happened.
> *"I pulled and pulled until a treat fell out. Proud. Very proud."*
> When someone walks in, Laika greets them, and that goes in the diary too.

**45–65 s: who it's for**

> It's for anyone whose dog is home alone all day.
> The dog gets a few simple ways to ask for a walk, a game or a treat.
> You get a short diary every evening. You'll read it every night, and send it to a friend.

**65–85 s: the new capability**

> Claude Opus 5.5 reads messy home-camera frames, corrects the tracker, and writes warmly without making anything up.
> It even returns which moments it used.

**85–90 s: the link**

> It's all on GitHub: **github.com/mauryapg13/Laika-A-Pet-Buddy**. Thank you.

⏱ **Hard stop at 90 s.**
